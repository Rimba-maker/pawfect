# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack
Astro 6 (SSG) + React 19 islands + Tailwind CSS v4 + Framer Motion + lucide-react. Existing codebase; keep it. Deploy target in the PRD is Netlify (static).

## Users
Indonesian pet parents (urban Jakarta, BSD, Bandung, middle-to-upper income) who treat dogs and cats as family and are active in pet communities on Instagram and TikTok. Jobs: book a grooming slot, find a safe boarding/daycare for a trip, buy premium food and gear, or enrol a puppy in training. Owners of breed-sensitive pets who need a specialist groomer. They arrive mostly on phones, often from social links, and finish by booking through a form or WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose
Pawfect Pet Studio is a one-stop pet studio: premium grooming, boarding and hotel, daycare, training, in-house vet consultation, a curated shop, and a membership (Pawfect Club). The site exists to make a pet parent trust the studio with their animal and book (grooming first, boarding and training next). Success = a booking request or a WhatsApp message.

## Positioning
Not a pet shop: a full-service "studio" where the same trusted team handles spa, stay, school and supplies, and knows each pet by name. A generic pet shop or a single-service groomer cannot truthfully claim the whole chain under one roof with personal recognition ("staff already know Luna's habits").

## Operating Context
Drop-off and pick-up model. Grooming takes 2-4 hours, boarding needs 24 hours advance booking, vaccination (DHPP/F3-F4/rabies) is mandatory for boarding. Hours Tuesday-Sunday 09:00-20:00, closed Monday. In-house vet Monday-Saturday daytime only; 24/7 emergencies referred to a partner clinic. Delivery via GoSend, GrabExpress, Tiki. Booking confirmation within one working hour.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Services: Basic Bath, Full Grooming, Spa Premium, breed-specific cuts (dogs, cats, rabbits, other pets); Standard / Comfort / Royal boarding suites; daycare (half and full day); Puppy Class, Obedience, Behavior Modification (force-free, positive reinforcement); vet consultation; shop (food, treats and supplements, toys, comfort and accessories, grooming products); Pawfect Club membership (Basic / Plus / VIP).
- Booking form fields: owner name, WhatsApp, email, pet name, breed and size, service, branch, preferred date. WhatsApp direct is the alternative.
- Content is Bahasa Indonesia. Currency Rp.
- Static site, no backend: booking form and shop cart are front-end only for now (no real checkout or submission endpoint decided).
- Undecided: real branch addresses, real phone number, real social handles, real vet and groomer identities.

## Brand Commitments
None locked. The user explicitly released name, tagline, copy, palette, typography and layout for a total redesign. The original PRD voice (playful, warm, caring, slightly cheeky; avoid clinical vet tone) is a starting reference, not binding.

## Evidence on Hand
This is a demo/portfolio project. Everything in the PRD is sample content: the 5,000+ pets figure, 5 branches, groomer names and certifications (Ms. Linda, Pak Reza and others), the four testimonials, prices and membership tiers. It must be treated as synthetic and labelled so wherever a visitor could take it for fact. No real customer photos, no real certifications, no real press. Photography is stock (Pexels / Unsplash) and must be credited; it depicts generic animals and people, never claimed as Pawfect's own staff or customers.

## Product Principles
1. The pet is the protagonist: every section should make a pet parent feel their animal is known and safe, not processed.
2. Reduce booking friction: from any section, a booking or WhatsApp action is one tap away on mobile.
3. Be honest about what is sample: no invented credentials, counts or reviews presented as real.
4. Premium without clinical: warmth and personality over medical or retail-catalogue coldness.
5. One studio, one chain: services connect (groom, stay, train, shop) rather than read as six separate businesses.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Mobile-first (most visitors arrive from Instagram/TikTok on phones). Text contrast WCAG AA, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion respected, alt text on all photography. Indonesian-language copy; avoid emoji as the only carrier of meaning.
